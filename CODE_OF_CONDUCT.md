# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

Contributors and maintainers of **Agent Token Receipt** commit to making participation a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background or experience level.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and patient in issues, pull requests, and reviews.
- Critique technical decisions and code—never individuals.
- Respect privacy boundaries: never request or share another person's private prompts, credentials, or session transcripts.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, or intimidation.
- Publishing private information, API keys, or confidential logs without permission.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may close threads, remove offending comments, or restrict participation for behavior that violates these standards. Report issues through repository maintainers via GitHub.
